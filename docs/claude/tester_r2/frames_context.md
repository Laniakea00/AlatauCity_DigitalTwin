Trial quarter of Gate district, Alatau City (Kazakhstan), year 2050 unless the frame name says otherwise. Area x −4200…−1700, z −20600…−18300 (Unity metres).
It contains: Iconic tower and the round Gate District quarter (existing design, moved to (−3500, −19500)); the A-3 × BAKAD cloverleaf and a new partial cloverleaf; a frontage road; an LRT line with an elevated section and stations; an existing village with gardens; schools, a park; Griffin logistics sheds with truck docks and yards; an agglomeration rail transit hub with a bus station under a canopy; an existing farm building kept with its own access street.
Frames t01–t10 are fixed views, r1–r5 random views, y20xx year views, constr_* construction mode (yellow = under construction, red = late), ground_* close-ups of the ground, tpu_* the transit hub, griffin_* the logistics docks and yard, piers_* bridge piers, lamps_* street lamps, plinth_* buildings on a slope, iconic_plaza the plaza around Iconic, barn_access the kept farm building. The camera line for each frame is in shots_plan.txt next to the frames (view x z distance yaw pitch).
